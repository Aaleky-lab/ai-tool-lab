## Implementation rules

1. Never replace an official URL until the application has been approved and the destination has been tested.
2. Store affiliate URLs in `tools.js`; do not scatter them across pages.
3. Use `rel="sponsored noopener noreferrer"` on affiliate destinations.
4. Keep `affiliate-disclosure.html` linked from every commercial guide and the site footer.
5. Do not publish commission percentages copied from directories. Use the accepted program dashboard or official terms.
6. Track outbound clicks by tool, but never collect personal data without an appropriate consent and privacy setup.
