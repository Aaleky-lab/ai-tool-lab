## Short 01 — The four-tool starter stack

**Hook:** “You do not need ten AI subscriptions to make faceless videos.”

Script: Use ChatGPT or Claude for the script, ElevenLabs for narration, Canva for visual scenes and CapCut for the final edit. That four-tool stack is enough to publish. Add specialist tools only when a real bottleneck appears. The full workflow is on AI Tool Lab.

## Short 02 — ChatGPT vs Claude

**Hook:** “Which is better for YouTube scripts: ChatGPT or Claude?”

Script: ChatGPT is the flexible all-rounder. Claude is especially strong for long context and careful prose. Give both the same brief, allow one revision, then choose the draft that needs less editing. The winner is the one that saves you time — not the one with the flashiest first paragraph.

## Short 03 — The biggest faceless-video mistake

**Hook:** “The biggest AI video mistake happens before you open the editor.”

Script: Creators copy an AI research answer straight into the script. Instead, use Perplexity to discover sources, open the original pages and verify every important claim. AI should speed up research — not replace evidence.
