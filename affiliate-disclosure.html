Transparency

Affiliate disclosure

AI Tool Lab may earn a commission when a visitor follows an eligible link and purchases a product. This never increases the visitor’s price.

How recommendations are chosen

Tools are selected for practical usefulness, audience fit and the clarity of their use case. Affiliate availability does not determine ratings, inclusion or editorial conclusions.

Current link status

At launch, provider buttons use direct official links unless an approved affiliate relationship has been added. When an affiliate link is used, it receives the appropriate sponsored link attribute.

What readers should verify

AI products change quickly. Pricing, free plans, credit limits, commercial-use rules and affiliate terms can change after publication. Always review the provider’s current terms before purchasing or using generated content commercially.

Editorial standard

AI Tool Lab aims to describe where a tool fits, where it does not and what a user should test before paying. Corrections should be made when a material claim becomes outdated.
