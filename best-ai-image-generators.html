Image guide

Best AI image generators

Different visual tools win at different jobs: expressive art, readable type, precise assets or polished everyday design.

Updated September 2026 · 6 minute read

Best picks by use case

  • Art direction and style: MidjourneyBest for distinctive concepts, moodboards and imaginative visual language.
  • Flexible asset creation: Leonardo AIA practical balance of controls for products, game assets and creative production.
  • Typography in images: IdeogramA strong starting point for posters, graphics and concepts that need readable text.
  • Adobe workflows: Adobe FireflyUseful when generation needs to fit into Photoshop and the wider Creative Cloud.
  • Design and delivery: CanvaThe easiest path from a generated idea to a finished thumbnail, post or presentation.

Generation is only half the workflow

An image model creates raw material. Most publishable work still needs cropping, layout, typography, brand colors and export settings. That is why Canva or Adobe can be more valuable than a visually stronger generator when the final deliverable matters more than experimentation.

How to compare quality

Use the same brief in every tool and score four things: prompt accuracy, consistency across variations, text rendering and the effort required to make a revision. Also check the provider’s current commercial-use rules before using generated assets for a client or product.

Our recommendation

Choose Midjourney for the strongest art-directed exploration, Ideogram when type is central, Leonardo for flexible asset production and Firefly when you already live in Adobe. Choose Canva when speed and finishing are the priority.

Free credits, plan limits and licensing terms change. Verify the current rules on the provider’s site.
