Best AI writing tools

Choose between a flexible assistant, a focused editor and a system built for repeatable marketing output.

Updated September 2026 · 6 minute read

Best picks by use case

  • Flexible everyday writing: ChatGPTUseful for outlines, drafts, rewrites, brainstorming and adapting tone.
  • Long documents and careful revision: ClaudeA strong fit for context-heavy work and thoughtful long-form editing.
  • Clarity and polish: GrammarlyBest when you already have a draft and want cleaner, more confident communication.
  • Marketing operations: JasperDesigned for brand voice, campaigns and repeatable marketing workflows.
  • Research before writing: PerplexityUseful for finding starting sources and mapping a topic before drafting.

Assistant, editor or platform?

ChatGPT and Claude can help across the full writing process. Grammarly is narrower but fits naturally into existing documents. Jasper makes most sense when a business needs consistent brand context and repeated campaign production rather than occasional writing help.

A safer writing workflow

Use AI to outline, challenge weak logic and create alternatives. Keep factual claims tied to sources, remove invented detail and edit the final piece in your own voice. Sensitive or confidential material should only be uploaded when the tool and your organization’s policy permit it.

Our recommendation

Most individuals should start by comparing ChatGPT and Claude on the same real task. Add Grammarly if editing is the main pain point. Consider Jasper only when the repeatability of a marketing system justifies another subscription.
