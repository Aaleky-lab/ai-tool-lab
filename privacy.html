Privacy

Your analytics choice

CEVYRA uses optional analytics only after a visitor gives permission.

What is measured

If you select “Allow analytics,” Google Analytics records visits, page views, scrolling and outbound link clicks. CEVYRA does not intentionally send names, email addresses or other directly identifying information to Google Analytics.

Consent and storage

Analytics is disabled by default. Your choice is stored in your browser’s local storage under ai-tool-lab-analytics-consent. You can withdraw or reset your choice at any time by clearing this site’s stored data in your browser.

Service provider

Analytics data is processed by Google under the Google Analytics terms. Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Affiliate links

CEVYRA may use clearly disclosed affiliate links. The current link status and editorial approach are described in the affiliate disclosure.
